ResidentHub app (prototype) - run it in Android Studio
=======================================================
1. Open Android Studio > File > Open > choose THIS folder (the one with pubspec.yaml).
2. Wait for "pub get" to finish (or open pubspec.yaml and click "Pub get").
3. At the top, pick a device (an Android emulator, or your phone with USB debugging on).
4. Press Run (green triangle). This copy of the app is the prototype by default, so no extra setting is needed.
5. On the sign-in screen tap "Continue as Administrator" or "Continue as Resident".
   Administrator: admin@demo.test  |  Resident: resident@demo.test  |  Password: Demo@1234

The prototype has no database. All the data is sample data kept in memory and resets when the app restarts.
This prototype copy always uses sample data. (To force it off for a test: --dart-define=PROTOTYPE=false.)

New in this version
- Finance: monthly dues per sqm (floor area x rate), "billed on" date on every charge, residents are notified,
  Daily collections chart, Day by day, Daily expenses, Unit dues table (10 per page), edit a charge amount.
- Units: floor area (sqm).
- Polls: polls and elections, five question types, formatted text (bold, italic, underline, bullets, large),
  categories (add, rename, delete), who voted, export results to Excel.
